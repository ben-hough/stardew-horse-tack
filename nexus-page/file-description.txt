Horse Tack & Styling 1.4.0 (main file). Unzip into Stardew Valley/Mods so you get Mods/HorseTack/manifest.json. Requires SMAPI 4.x and Stardew Valley 1.6 (built and tested with SMAPI 4.5.2 on Stardew 1.6.15, Windows). Includes 57 original pieces. Elle's Cuter Horses and Generic Mod Config Menu are optional. Remove Multiplayer Horse Reskin (it conflicts). In multiplayer every player needs the same version and matching optional art.
