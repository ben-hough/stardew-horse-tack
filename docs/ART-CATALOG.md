## Known weak spots (next art pass)
- Luau lei reads as thin dots; Junimo buddy reads more like a sprout (small eyes).
- Pads show only as an edge in the down-facing view.
- Elle's pads over HorseTack saddles can overlap (our pads cut out the saddle footprint; hers don't).
- Rosette + crown together get busy on the eating frames.
