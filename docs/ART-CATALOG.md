### 1.4.0 additions: Forest Spirit (8 pieces)
An original woodland set in HorseTack's own style (generic forest motifs; nothing taken from any other mod; no moon, no elk shapes):
- **Spirit Coat**: pale sage-grey dappled coat, lichen-pale mane with moss streaks, faint glowing freckles.
- **Heartwood** saddle: carved wood with growth rings on the seat, rough bark rims and a living sprout on the pommel.
- **Root & Stone** saddle: smooth stone-grey leather with winding roots and moss tufts.
- **Moss Cloak** pad (seasonal): a longer mossy drape with ferns and uneven moss strands hanging below the belly, and flaps that show at the shoulders from the front.
- **Glowing Runes** bridle: dark leather with glowing cyan rune dots and a small glyph on the face.
- **Vine** bridle: leafy vine straps with tiny white, pink and yellow flowers.
- **Antler Crown** styling (seasonal): a twig circlet with two short branching deer antlers and leaves, drawn for side, eating, front, back and icon frames.
- **Wisp Halo** styling: four tiny glowing fireflies/wisps around the head that bob a pixel from frame to frame.

Seasonal trims (Moss Cloak and Antler Crown): the plain file is the summer look (green leaves, tiny yellow flowers); `.spring` has pink and white blossoms, `.fall` red-orange leaves, and `.winter` frost (pale blue fringe tips and frosted antler tips). The game's current season picks the file automatically.

Suggested looks: *forest spirit* = Spirit Coat + Heartwood + Moss Cloak + Vine + Antler Crown; *glade keeper* = Dapple Grey + Root & Stone + Moss Cloak + Glowing Runes + Wisp Halo.

### 1.4.0 art pass on existing pieces (ids unchanged)
- **All pads**: a short fringe below the lowest edge in every view, and longer shoulder flaps, so pads show below the belly and from the front. Pads are now drawn under saddles (see README), so they no longer leave a gap for Elle's saddle shape either.
- **Familiar**: a pale violet rim and gold eyes keep it readable on dark coats. From the back it now perches on the saddle's right side, looking back, instead of hiding behind the Broomstick bristles. From the front, its ears and eyes peek over the horse's back.
- **Moss & Mushroom**: real red-cap mushrooms with a white spot, dark gills and a cream stem.
- **Red Dun**: darker, two-pixel dorsal stripe and stronger leg barring.
- **Crescent Charm**: fully dark-outlined (holds up on Sabino and white coats) and now also on the small head icons.
- **Prismatic Pearl**: opal flecks (aqua, lilac, blush) on a regular lattice over the body; easy to see without being noisy.
- **Luau Lei**: chunky plus-shaped flowers with yellow centres and leaves.
- **Junimo Buddy**: a proper Junimo: star leaf on a stalk, round body, dot eyes, stubby arms and feet. From the back it perches on the saddle's side.
- **Blue Ribbon + Flower Dance Crown**: no ribbon tails and a smaller crown on the eating frames.

### 1.3.0 additions (22 pieces)
- **Breeds** (7 coats): original coats *inspired by real horse colours* (bay with a silver mane, red dun with dorsal stripe and leg barring, grulla, dapple grey, brindle, sabino with blaze/white legs/belly, gold champagne with pink-toned muzzle and sheen). Painted on the vanilla body by region (head/legs/body) from our own palettes; not affiliated with Elle's packs and not drawn from her sprites.
- **Prismatic** (2 coats): *Night* (near-black with a violet sheen and prismatic glints on the body highlights; the mane and tail are one smooth aurora gradient, teal to violet to magenta, with star glints) and *Pearl* (pearl white with pastel shimmer highlights and a silver mane with mother-of-pearl flecks). Deliberately no banded rainbow mane.
- **Ranch**: Sage saddle (stitched sage-green leather) and matching Sage bridle (brass buckles).
- **Winter**: Frostglass saddle (icy glass panels) and Frostglass pad (frost-fern pattern).
- **Witchy** (1 coat + 7 overlays), an original set to go with a witchy farm (style inspiration only; nothing taken from any other mod): Broomstick saddle (dark wood with a straw-bristle tail at the back), Grimoire saddle (leather-bound book with a brass clasp, ribbon and corners), Moss & Mushroom pad (mossy green with red-cap mushrooms), Starry Hex pad (deep purple with silver moon/star glyphs), Potion Vials bridle (tiny coloured vials on the cheek strap), Crescent Charm bridle (silver crescent pendant at the throat latch plus a small crescent on the noseband), Witch Hat styling (pointed hat between the ears, drawn for side, eating, front and back frames), Familiar styling (tiny black cat riding behind the saddle), Midnight Familiar coat (near-black with a violet sheen). Palette: deep purples, moss green, mushroom red, aged brass, moon silver.

Suggested looks: *full witchy* = Midnight Familiar + Broomstick + Starry Hex + Crescent Charm + Witch Hat; *hedge witch* = Grulla + Broomstick + Moss & Mushroom + Potion Vials + Witch Hat; *scholar* = Dapple Grey + Grimoire + Starry Hex + Crescent Charm + Familiar.

## Known weak spots (next art pass)
- Styles draw under saddles, so the Antler Crown's antlers are hidden by the saddle in the front view (only the circlet and leaves show on the forehead there); side, back and icon views show them.
- Seasonal trims are small accents (a few leaf/blossom/frost pixels); the season reads best on the Moss Cloak fringe (winter) and antler tips.
- The Moss Cloak covers a lot of the barrel; on the green-grey Spirit Coat everything is quite green (try it on bays/greys).
- The Familiar and Junimo sit on the saddle's side in the back view, not behind it (styles can't draw over the saddle).
- Red Dun leg barring is still faint on the darker legs.
- Pads' front flaps are small (the horse's head covers the chest in the front view).
